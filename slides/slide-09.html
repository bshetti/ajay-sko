Net-new VS existing customers

Do we always start with Serverless?

Net-New Prospects

The Default Path: Always lead with Elastic Cloud Serverless for new logos.

The Focus: Fastest time to value, least operational burden, and continuous innovation.

Existing Customers

The Strategy: Upsell based on operational relief, not a mandatory migration.

The Trigger: Target teams bogged down by capacity planning, manual version upgrades, or wasted cloud spend.
